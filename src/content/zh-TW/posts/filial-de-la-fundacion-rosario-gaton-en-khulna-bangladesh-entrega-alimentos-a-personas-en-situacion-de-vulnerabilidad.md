**孟加拉國庫爾納。**
**Fundación Rosario Gatón** 透過其位於**孟加拉國庫爾納**的分會，開展了一場針對極度弱勢群體的糧食發放活動，再次彰顯了其對人道主義事業及弱勢群體福祉的全球承諾。在此次愛心行動中，當地分會的志工們向許多受貧困、失業或社會遺棄所苦的家庭與無助民眾分發了口糧。這項介入行動響應了聯合國所推動的**「消除飢餓」永續發展目標（SDG 2）**，也是 Fundación Rosario Gatón 持續擴展國際觸角、跨越多明尼加共和國國界支援社區的努力之一。**Fundación Rosario Gatón 庫爾納分會**是該組織蓬勃發展的生動見證，他們不懈地為人類尊嚴、包容性發展以及在最需要的地區提供具體援助而努力。總部對促成此次發放活動的志工與社區領袖們的努力表達了誠摯的感謝，並傳遞了一個明確的訊息：*「團結互助，地理無界。」* ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/119510794_3058063457626141_3925894108112061703_n.png) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/119503533_3058060590959761_2141002445055085828_n.png) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/119157672_3058061820959638_5486343917342965581_n.png) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/119116855_3058062714292882_3649491998564365596_n.png) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/119114123_3058063047626182_302507697780677596_n.png) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/119099338_3058061314293022_6951537108766425304_n.png) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/119080893_3058062334292920_341934461004559705_n.png) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/119072674_3058064374292716_1156553516868989116_n.png) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/119070094_3058063867626100_8639727286493159552_n.png) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/119061000_3058059977626489_518822333427617714_n.png) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/119054607_3058059200959900_1964614313946877378_n.png) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/119037367_3058066080959212_1519807293402683179_n.png)
