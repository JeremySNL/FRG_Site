La **Fundación Rosario Gatón (FRG)** 持續展現對弱勢族群健康、飲食與尊嚴的承諾。在又一次的愛心行動中，本機構按照人道援助計畫的規畫，向**多位家庭經濟支柱發放了糧食包裹**。這些發放行動是社區援助綜合策略的一部分，旨在改善弱勢及資源匱乏民眾的生活品質。每一包糧食傳遞的不僅是食物，更是希望、團結與人道支持。FRG 透過實際行動重申其對聯合國**永續發展目標 (SDGs)** 的承諾，特別是**目標 2：消除飢餓**與**目標 3：健康與福祉**，為鞏固家庭作為建立公平正義社會的基石貢獻心力。 ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/153419210_3486246501474499_3389036132229290343_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152942829_3486246518141164_811300107023893931_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152735183_3486246498141166_4139285619324287667_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152714597_3486246488141167_1582936361189586119_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152691940_3486246508141165_6965434894000257240_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152581537_3486246504807832_6978881936437505171_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152546864_3486246514807831_8549730807493528115_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152327757_3486246494807833_242944154639295931_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152194905_3486246511474498_7849458473572496383_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152194905_3486246491474500_1094128402586501886_n.jpg)
