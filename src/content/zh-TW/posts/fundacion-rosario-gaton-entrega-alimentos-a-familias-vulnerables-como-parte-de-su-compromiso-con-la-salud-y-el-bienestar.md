在國內不同的社區中，**Fundación Rosario Gatón (FRG)** 透過向**處於弱勢狀況的家庭分發食品和基本生活用品**，持續展現其對社會福祉的承諾。這場愛心關懷行動惠及了數十個以婦女、長者及高風險族群為主的家庭，他們收到了旨在**改善營養與生活品質**的食品包。本基金會秉持聯合國**「永續發展目標」（SDGs）**的精神，特別是**目標 2：消除飢餓**與**目標 3：健康與福祉**，積極為確保所有人都能獲得充足食物作出貢獻。 ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/192499427_3779589355473544_7932872953154921549_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/193226807_3779589345473545_5041561472688224496_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/193288608_3779589348806878_8869084500417185872_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/194268476_3779589365473543_5903311271533166036_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/194482025_3779589372140209_6102377695419728770_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/194769352_3779589362140210_1311684501893869371_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/194838693_3779589368806876_6367547319717260429_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/194865708_3779589342140212_8264192514272201068_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/196291111_3779589358806877_6869218971322080492_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/196853294_3779589352140211_2223174242837583812_n.jpg)
