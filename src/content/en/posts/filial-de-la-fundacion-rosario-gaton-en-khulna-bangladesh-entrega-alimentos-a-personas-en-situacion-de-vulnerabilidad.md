**Khulna, Bangladesh.**
**Fundación Rosario Gatón**, through its branch in **Khulna, Bangladesh**, carried out a food distribution drive aimed at individuals in situations of extreme vulnerability, reaffirming its global commitment to humanitarian causes and the well-being of the most disadvantaged. During this solidarity action, volunteers from the local branch distributed food rations among families and defenseless individuals, many of them affected by poverty, unemployment, or social abandonment. This intervention responds to the fulfillment of **Sustainable Development Goal No. 2: Zero Hunger**, promoted by the United Nations, and forms part of Fundación Rosario Gatón's ongoing effort to extend its international reach, supporting communities beyond Dominican borders. The **Fundación Rosario Gatón Filial Khulna** is a living testament to the growth of this organization, which works tirelessly for human dignity, inclusive development, and concrete aid where it is needed most.
