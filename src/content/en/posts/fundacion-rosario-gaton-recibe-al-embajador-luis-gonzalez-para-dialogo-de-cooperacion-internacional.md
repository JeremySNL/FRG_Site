Fundación Rosario Gatón was honored to receive the visit of **His Excellency Luis González**, Ambassador for Asia and Oceania, distinguished political scientist and internationalist, with whom high-level conversations focused on social development were held. During the meeting, key topics related to international cooperation, inclusion, and the strengthening of initiatives that positively impact citizens, especially in vulnerable sectors, were addressed. This visit strengthens diplomatic relations and the foundation's commitment to strategic collaboration for sustainable development.
