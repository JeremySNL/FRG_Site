**Fundación Rosario Gatón and INFOTEP strengthen partnership for technical and social development** The **Fundación Rosario Gatón (FRG)** and the **Instituto Nacional de Formación Técnico Profesional (INFOTEP)** continue to strengthen their inter-institutional relations through agreements and strategic alliances aimed at fulfilling **Sustainable Development Goal No. 17**: *“Partnerships for the goals”*. This important meeting was attended, on behalf of INFOTEP, by **Eng. Henrry de Jesús Lizardo**, Head of the Satellite Office; and on behalf of Fundación Rosario Gatón, by top executives **Alba Berenice Rosario Gatón** and **Dr. Julio César Rosario Gatón**. This collaboration reaffirms the commitment of both institutions to technical training, capacity building, and the creation of opportunities to transform the lives of thousands of Dominicans.
