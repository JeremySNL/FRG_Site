**Fundación Rosario Gatón holds its 2019 General Assembly in San Francisco de Macorís** **San Francisco de Macorís, República Dominicana – March 17, 2019.**
**Fundación Rosario Gatón (FRG)** held its **General Assembly** with the active participation of most of its key leaders, with the purpose of strengthening institutional unity and defining strategies for the execution of ongoing projects. During the session, the progress achieved in the first three months of management was evaluated, and it was agreed to jointly promote actions that will benefit communities both nationally and internationally. This assembly reaffirms the foundation's commitment to the United Nations **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** and the Dominican State's **National Development Strategy (END)**, in its mission to transform lives through social, educational, and human work.
