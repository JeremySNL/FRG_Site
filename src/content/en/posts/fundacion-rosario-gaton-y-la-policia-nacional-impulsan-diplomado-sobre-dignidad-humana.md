**La Policía Nacional and Fundación Rosario Gatón coordinate diploma course on Human Dignity in San Francisco de Macorís** The **Policía Nacional Dominicana**, under the leadership of **General Olivance Minaya**, and **Fundación Rosario Gatón**, represented by its Founding President, **Dr. Julio César Rosario Gatón**, held a meeting to coordinate the implementation of the diploma course entitled **"Human Dignity"**, to be taught soon in **San Francisco de Macorís**. This training program will be developed by the law enforcement body of the Policía Nacional and seeks to strengthen human values, institutional ethics, and civic awareness among participants. This initiative represents an important step in the promotion of human rights and civic education, in alignment with the vision of both institutions.
