Chen Lunig Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of the People's Republic of China in the República Dominicana Within the framework of a prominent day of geopolitical analysis and international cooperation, His Excellency Mr. Luis González, former Ambassador to Asia and Oceania, distinguished Internationalist Political Scientist and renowned International Business Consultant, masterfully fulfilled his role as host by extending a first-class invitation to Dr. Julio César Rosario Gatón, president of the Fundación Rosario Gatón, and to the vice president, Mr. Don Pedro Eugenio Ángeles, who had the honor of actively participating in the significant lecture delivered by His Excellency Mr. Chen Lunig, Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of the People's Republic of China in the República Dominicana.
