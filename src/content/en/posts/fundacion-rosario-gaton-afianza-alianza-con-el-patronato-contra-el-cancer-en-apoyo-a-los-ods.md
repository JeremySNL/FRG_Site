**Fundación Rosario Gatón strengthens ties with the Patronato Contra el Cáncer in San Francisco de Macorís** San Francisco de Macorís, República Dominicana.
In compliance with the United Nations **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**, especially **Goal No. 17: “Partnerships for the goals”**, the **Fundación Rosario Gatón (FRG)** held an institutional meeting with two distinguished representatives of the **Patronato Contra el Cáncer** in the northeastern region. The meeting was attended by **Her Excellency Ambassador Lic. Socorro Vargas**, Executive Director of the Patronato, and **Her Excellency Ambassador Lic. Yeraldin E. Payano Meyreles**, Director of the Marketing Department. This strategic alliance marks a firm step toward interinstitutional collaboration and seeks to pave the way for the Foundation's partners, allies, and future members to have the backing of a network committed to health, inclusion, and human rights.
