**Fundación Rosario Gatón** is pleased to officially announce that it has been recognized in the **2026 edition of the CSR Awards** (Corporate Social Responsibility Awards), a distinction granted annually by the prestigious international publication *Acquisition International*. Our organization has been chosen as the winner in the category:

> **Best Poverty Reduction & Social Inclusion Foundation 2026 – Dominican Republic**

![CSR Awards - Acquisition International](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcQ09Kd0eIQBjQxwezyf8MpzS0pxRL0nL-4obXpRppQmoQ&s=10)

## Recognition for Social Impact in the Dominican Republic

This international award highlights the ongoing work, rigor, and deep commitment of **Fundación Rosario Gatón** to implementing projects aimed at reducing poverty, fostering social inclusion, and creating opportunities for vulnerable communities in the Dominican Republic. Through programs focused on community development, education, and direct social support, the Foundation has worked actively to generate sustainable transformations that improve the quality of life of those who need it most.

![Fundación Rosario Gatón and Cruz Jiminián join efforts to strengthen social action](https://francomacorisanos.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/WhatsApp-Image-2026-06-18-at-3.08.38-PM-4.jpeg)

## Reaffirming Our Commitment to the Future

Upon receiving this distinction, the Institutional Directorate of Fundación Rosario Gatón reaffirms its commitment to continue expanding the reach of its initiatives and strengthening strategic alliances at the national and international levels.

> *"This award motivates us to keep working with greater dedication. We reiterate our responsibility to drive actions that create a positive, fair, and lasting impact on society."*

## Acknowledgments

We sincerely thank *Acquisition International* for this important recognition, as well as all our allies, volunteers, goodwill ambassadors, and collaborators whose daily effort makes it possible to fulfill our mission.
