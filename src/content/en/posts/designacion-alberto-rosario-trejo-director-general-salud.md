**SANTO DOMINGO, R.D.** – The Fundación Rosario Gatón officially announced the appointment of Dr. Alberto Rosario Trejo as Director General of the institution's Health Department. This appointment represents a significant step in strengthening the healthcare services offered by the organization.

The appointment was announced during a working meeting held at the Centro Médico Dr. Ovalle, where important topics were addressed regarding initiatives that will directly benefit the population and the community members who receive medical care.

## A new stage for the Health Department

The meeting was attended by the Founding President of the Fundación Rosario Gatón, Dr. Julio César Rosario Gatón, with whom the strategic lines of action for the new health management were discussed.

Also participating:

- **Dr. Alberto Rosario Trejo**, new Director General of the Health Department.
- **Don Luilly Paulino Lantigua**, Director of the Agriculture Department.
- **Doña Agustina Medina Herrera**, Coordinator of Health Management and Liaison.

## Commitment to community well-being

During the session, those present reaffirmed their commitment to working in a coordinated and interinstitutional manner to ensure that health and well-being programs reach the communities most in need efficiently.

With this appointment, the Fundación Rosario Gatón continues to drive the strengthening of its institutional structure and to consolidate the comprehensive development it promotes in the region.
