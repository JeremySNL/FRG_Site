La **Fundación Rosario Gatón (FRG)** continues to demonstrate its commitment to the health, nutrition, and dignity of those in greatest need. In a new solidarity outreach, **food rations were delivered to various heads of households**, as part of the humanitarian support program promoted by the institution. These distributions are part of a comprehensive community aid strategy aimed at improving the quality of life for individuals in vulnerable conditions and with limited resources. With each bag of food, more than just nourishment is delivered: hope, solidarity, and human support are provided. La FRG reaffirms through its actions its commitment to the United Nations **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**, especially **SDG 2: Zero Hunger** and **SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being**, contributing to the strengthening of families as the foundation of a just and equitable society. ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/153419210_3486246501474499_3389036132229290343_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152942829_3486246518141164_811300107023893931_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152735183_3486246498141166_4139285619324287667_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152714597_3486246488141167_1582936361189586119_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152691940_3486246508141165_6965434894000257240_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152581537_3486246504807832_6978881936437505171_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152546864_3486246514807831_8549730807493528115_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152327757_3486246494807833_242944154639295931_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152194905_3486246511474498_7849458473572496383_n.jpg) ![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/152194905_3486246491474500_1094128402586501886_n.jpg)
