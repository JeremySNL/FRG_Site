**Santo Domingo, República Dominicana.** **Fundación Rosario Gatón**, in collaboration with the **Cámara Americana de Comercio de la República Dominicana (AMCHAMDR)**, continues to build bridges and create opportunities for the growth of its members, affiliates, and allied communities. Within this framework of cooperation, the visit of **Mr. Gildel R. Mateo**, Provincial Coordinator of AMCHAMDR, was announced, with whom new avenues will be explored to promote sustainable development initiatives, aligned with the **Estrategia Nacional de Desarrollo (END)** of the Dominican State and the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). This alliance reaffirms our commitment to working alongside the private sector and the international community to expand the economic, social, and educational opportunities of the population.
