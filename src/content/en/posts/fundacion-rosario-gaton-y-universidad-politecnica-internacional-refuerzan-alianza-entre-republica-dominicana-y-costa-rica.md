**República Dominicana and Costa Rica strengthen international cooperation through Fundación Rosario Gatón and Universidad Politécnica Internacional** **Fundación Rosario Gatón (FRG)** and **Universidad Politécnica Internacional** have joined forces to drive a strategic alliance that reinforces compliance with United Nations **Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) No. 17**: *"Partnerships for the goals"*. This agreement promotes education, institutional strengthening, and academic exchange between both countries, as part of the commitment to the **Estrategia Nacional de Desarrollo (END)** of the Dominican State and global development objectives. We thank **Msc. Abel Jiménez Obando**, Academic Director of Law at Universidad Politécnica Internacional, and **Ambassador Dr. Lucy Jiménez**, General Coordinator of FRG in Costa Rica, for their vision, commitment, and leadership in building this bridge of international cooperation.
