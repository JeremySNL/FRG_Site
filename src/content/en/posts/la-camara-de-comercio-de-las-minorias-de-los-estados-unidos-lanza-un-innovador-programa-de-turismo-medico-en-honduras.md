![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/WhatsApp-Image-2026-02-12-at-4.17.50-PM-200x300.jpeg) Washington D.C. —February 9, 2026. In response to the growing need for access to high-quality and affordable healthcare services for minority communities in the United States, the United States Minority Chamber of Commerce (USMCC) officially announces the launch of its new Medical Tourism Program in Honduras. This strategic initiative seeks to offer first-class medical services, backed by board-certified physicians, accredited clinics, and professional liability insurance, all within an accessible and safe environment. The program includes a wide range of specialties: Complex surgeries Dental procedures Plastic and cosmetic surgery Orthopedics and cardiovascular treatments Rehabilitation and postoperative services Ophthalmology In addition to the medical component, the program integrates complementary services that guarantee a comprehensive experience for participants: lodging (hotels and hostels), internal transportation, as well as entertainment and tourism activities, featuring guided tours that highlight the natural and cultural beauty of the Central American country. “The goal is to provide our members with safe, reliable, and economical healthcare alternatives while they enjoy the charm and hospitality that Honduras offers,” stated Doug Mayorga, President of the USMCC. “This project represents an innovative solution for those seeking specialized medical care at reasonable prices without compromising quality.” Why Honduras?: Now is the time to take advantage of this program where science, technology, and innovation have been extraordinary in the Central American country, especially today, as a new government has won election that needs support to energize, diversify, and consolidate the economy in this service sector, and establish a global presence in this field.
