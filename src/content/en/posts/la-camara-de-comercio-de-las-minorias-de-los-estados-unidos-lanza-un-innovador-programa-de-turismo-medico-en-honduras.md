![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/WhatsApp-Image-2026-02-12-at-4.17.50-PM-200x300.jpeg) Washington D.C. — February 9, 2026. In response to the growing need for access to high-quality, low-cost healthcare services for minority communities in the United States, the Cámara de Comercio de las Minorías de los Estados Unidos (USMCC) officially announces the launch of its new Medical Tourism Program in Honduras. This strategic initiative seeks to offer first-class medical services, backed by certified physicians, accredited clinics, and professional liability insurance, all within an accessible and secure environment. The program includes a wide range of specialties: Complex surgeries Dental procedures Aesthetic and cosmetic surgery Orthopedics and cardiovascular treatments Rehabilitation and postoperative services Ophthalmology In addition to the medical component, the program integrates complementary services that guarantee a comprehensive experience for participants: lodging (hotels and hostels), internal transportation, as well as entertainment and tourism activities, with guided tours highlighting the natural and cultural beauty of the Central American country. “The goal is to provide our members with safe, reliable, and affordable healthcare alternatives, while they enjoy the charm and hospitality offered by Honduras,” stated Doug Mayorga, President of the USMCC. “This project represents an innovative solution for those seeking specialized medical care at reasonable prices without compromising quality.” Why Honduras?: Now is the time to take advantage of this program where science, technology, and innovation have been extraordinary in the Central American country, especially today, as a new government has won the election that needs support to stimulate, diversify, and consolidate the economy in this service sector, and establish a global presence in this field.
