In an emotional event of cooperation and social commitment, renowned physician and philanthropist **Dr. Félix Antonio Cruz Jiminián** offered words filled with sensitivity and hope during the signing of a collaboration agreement with the **Fundación Rosario Gatón (FRG)**. During the event, Dr. Cruz Jiminián expressed his admiration for the work carried out by the Foundation on behalf of those in most need, highlighting that *“solidarity is the most powerful medicine to heal the social wounds of our country”*. He also reiterated his willingness to join forces to reach more people with medical care, inclusion programs, and humanitarian aid. The agreement signed between both institutions seeks to develop **joint actions in health, education, social assistance, and care for vulnerable sectors**, thereby reinforcing the impact of community initiatives throughout the national territory. The Fundación Rosario Gatón, represented by its Founding President, **Dr. Julio César Rosario Gatón**, deeply appreciated this historic step, which unites two entities with the same purpose: **to serve with love, commitment, and concrete action**. This agreement is part of the United Nations **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** and the **National Development Strategy (END)** of the Dominican State, strengthening the social fabric through joint work.
