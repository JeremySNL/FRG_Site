Santo Domingo, República Dominicana- Senior executives of the Fundación Rosario Gatón (FRG) held a meaningful meeting with national authorities, including Lic. Rudy Reynoso, Major of the National Police and Director of the School of Human Rights of the Police Education Institute (IPE), alongside other distinguished members of that prestigious institution. During the meeting, the joint commitment to promote actions aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and the National Development Strategy (END) of the Dominican State was reaffirmed. This important step is part of the strategic actions of the FRG to strengthen the country's institutions and actively contribute to the reduction of crime, gender violence, and extreme poverty, promoting a culture of peace, human rights, and social inclusion.
