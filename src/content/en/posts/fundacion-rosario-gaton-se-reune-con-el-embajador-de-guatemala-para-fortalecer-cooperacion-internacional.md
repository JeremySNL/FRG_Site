**Fundación Rosario Gatón strengthens diplomatic ties with the Embassy of Guatemala** **Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic – Embassy of Guatemala.**
**Fundación Rosario Gatón (FRG)** held an institutional meeting with the **Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of Guatemala, His Excellency Rudy Armando Coxaj López**, as part of its strategy for international expansion and the strengthening of interinstitutional cooperation. During the encounter, both parties reaffirmed their commitment to working toward the consolidation of bonds of fraternity, collaboration, and support among international branches and representations. This initiative is part of the ongoing efforts of FRG to fulfill the United Nations **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** and the **National Development Strategy (END)** of the Dominican State.
