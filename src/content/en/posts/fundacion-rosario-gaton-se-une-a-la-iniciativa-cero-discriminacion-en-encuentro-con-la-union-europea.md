**Fundación Rosario Gatón participates in a talk on "Zero Discrimination" alongside the European Union and the Vice Presidency of the Republic** **Santo Domingo, República Dominicana – Teatro Nacional.**
Representatives of **Fundación Rosario Gatón (FRG)** attended the talk on **"Zero Discrimination"**, held at the Teatro Nacional, following an official invitation from the **Vicepresidencia de la República Dominicana** and the **Comunidad Económica Europea**. During the event, an important meeting was held with **His Excellency Grippa**, Ambassador of the **European Union** to the country, discussing issues related to human rights, equality, and social inclusion. Participation in this activity reaffirms the Foundation's commitment to promoting a fairer, more equitable society free from all forms of discrimination.
