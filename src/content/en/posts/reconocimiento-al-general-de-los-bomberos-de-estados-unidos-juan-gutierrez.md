Dr. Julio César Rosario Gatón, president of the Fundación Rosario Gatón, and the Provincial Civil Governor of Duarte in San Francisco de Macorís, Lic. Ana Xiomara Cortés, welcomed on Friday, March 6 of this current year 2026, the Continental General Director of the World Fire Academy of the United States, Juan Gutiérrez, holding an important assembly with the objective of presenting and resuming cooperation agreements between the Fundación Rosario Gatón and the Lt. Gen. of the US Fire Department, Juan Gutiérrez. During the event, a recognition was presented to the Lt. Gen. of the US Fire Department, Juan Gutiérrez, for his outstanding and important work in favor of the most vulnerable sectors of our nation.
