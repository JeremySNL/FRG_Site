In various communities across the country, the **Fundación Rosario Gatón (FRG)** continues to demonstrate its commitment to social well-being through the delivery of **food and basic supplies to families in vulnerable situations**. This solidarity initiative benefited dozens of households headed by women, older adults, and individuals at risk, who received food rations with the purpose of **improving their nutrition and quality of life**.
