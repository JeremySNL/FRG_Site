Senior executives from **Fundación Rosario Gatón** participated in an event organized by the **Damas Diplomáticas**, held at the **Ministry of Defense of the República Dominicana**, where a meeting was held with **His Excellency Danny Biran**, Ambassador of Israel. During this institutional visit, topics of mutual interest were discussed, aimed at cooperation, the strengthening of diplomatic ties, and the development of joint initiatives to benefit the most vulnerable sectors. These types of outreach efforts reinforce the Foundation's commitment to international dialogue and collaboration for human and social development.
