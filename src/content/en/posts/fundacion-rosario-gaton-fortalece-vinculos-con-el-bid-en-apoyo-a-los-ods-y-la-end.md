**Luis Suárez, Secretary General of the Fundación Rosario Gatón, meets with senior BID executives to strengthen strategic alliances** The Secretary General of the **Fundación Rosario Gatón (FRG)**, **Luis Suárez**, held an important meeting with senior executives of the **Banco Interamericano de Desarrollo (BID)**, with the objective of establishing institutional cooperation ties and exploring joint development opportunities. During the meeting, initiatives aligned with the United Nations **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** and the **Estrategia Nacional de Desarrollo (END)** of the Dominican State were discussed, reaffirming the commitment to working together for the well-being of the most vulnerable communities. These alliances reaffirm the Foundation's active role in the regional arena as a promoter of sustainable human development.
