![](https://fundacionrosariogaton.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/WhatsApp-Image-2025-12-31-at-9.41.31-AM-300x225.jpeg) This Saturday, November 8, Dr. Cruz Jiminian received a delegation from the Fundación Rosario Gatón, led by its president, Dr. Julio Cesar Rosario Gatón, Vice President Pedro de los Angeles, and its Press Director, licensed professional and communicator Oxiris Reynoso. During the meeting, medical observations were conducted for two patients under the care of the Fundación Rosario Gatón and attended to by the honorable Galeano Cruz Jiminian, who work together for the well-being of the most vulnerable sectors.
