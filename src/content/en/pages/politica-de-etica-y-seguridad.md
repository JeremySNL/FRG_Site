This policy sets out the guidelines that govern the conduct of our members, collaborators, partners, and beneficiaries.</p><h3>Commitment to legality</h3><p>The foundation neither finances nor accepts any support, donation, or collaboration that is directly or indirectly linked to unlawful activities, such as:</p><ul><li>Money laundering</li><li>Terrorism financing</li><li>Drug trafficking</li><li>Human trafficking</li><li>Corruption and other criminal offenses</li></ul><p>Any institutional or individual relationship that violates these provisions will be suspended immediately.</p><hr /><h3>Ethical and responsible conduct</h3><p>All members, employees, volunteers, and representatives of FRG must act with honesty, respect, professionalism, and responsibility in carrying out their duties.</p><p>We are committed to:</p><ul><li>Fostering a safe, inclusive, and discrimination-free environment.</li><li>Ensuring the proper and transparent use of resources.</li><li>Respecting human rights as well as national and international legal frameworks.</li><li>Reporting any conduct or practice that violates this policy.</li></ul><hr /><h3>Protection of the people we serve</h3><p>We safeguard the physical, emotional, and moral integrity of everyone who takes part in our activities. We reject all forms of violence, abuse, harassment, or exploitation, particularly against people in situations of vulnerability.</p><hr /><h3>Reporting mechanism</h3><p>Anyone who becomes aware of acts contrary to this policy may reach out confidentially through:<br /><strong><a href=\"mailto:info@fundacionrosariogaton.org\">info@fundacionrosariogaton.org</a></strong></p><hr /><h3>Compliance and review</h3><p>This policy is mandatory. It is reviewed periodically to keep pace with new regulations, best practices, and institutional contexts.</p>"
